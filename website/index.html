How do I get my key?

Right after you pay, your key and the download link are sent to your email.

When does my 30 days start?

The first time you enter your key in game, not when you buy it.

Can I use it on another PC?

Each key is locked to one PC. Lifetime keys get a free reset every 3 months. Ask in Discord to move your key.

Which version do I need?

Minecraft 1.21 with Fabric Loader and Fabric API installed.

Do you give refunds?

Keys are delivered instantly, so sales are final. If something doesn't work, open a ticket and we'll fix it.
